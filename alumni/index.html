Alumni Association

Reconnecting past pupils of AIS, one memory and event at a time.

The Asian International School Alumni Association (AAA) is a go-to resource for all past pupils of AIS. Our aim is to foster a network and keep you in the know by providing information about AIS events and Alumni activities.

What are the benefits of joining?

There are many reasons to join the AAA. For one, we throw a fantastic party – ask any of your friends who attended our Launch Party & AGM on the 28th of December 2011.

Our aim is to provide a supportive network for all past pupils of AIS, from promoting the accomplishments of our members, to planning nostalgic and fun events for all of us to get together, and have a great time.

Furthermore, if you're a new alumni who has just recently graduated, we want to assist you in your career in any way we can, from connecting you with alumni in a field of your interest and helping you in your search for internships, to helping you plan for university in another country.

We look forward to seeing you at our next event!
