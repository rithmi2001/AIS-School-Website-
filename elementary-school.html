Elementary School

Where our youngest learners begin their joyful school journey.

Our well-resourced and creatively laid out Elementary School provides a happy, safe and a very homely environment where we gradually prepare your child for school life. Children from the ages of 2 ½ to 7 years learn and develop both academically and socially in a stimulating learning environment.

Our classrooms are designed to be inviting and inspiring with diverse subject areas in which the children are encouraged to explore independently. Age appropriate materials enhance the learning of literacy and numeracy skills, creative development and social skills. Learning through practical experience is an integral part of our curriculum.

Children are given many opportunities to explore and investigate. Each classroom has two full-time, qualified teachers who set the tone for an exciting classroom experience.

The Elementary School lays the foundation for a smooth transition to Grade 3.

Children are encouraged to participate in a variety of extra-curricular activities such as Art, Music, Speech and Drama, Dance and Swimming. Special events such as Children's Day celebrations, Independence Day celebrations, Sports meet, the Art and Craft exhibition and the Elementary School Christmas Drama production take place annually.
