Asian International School In Battaramulla

A warm and secure beginning for your child's educational journey.

Asian International School Battaramulla is committed to giving your precious child a rock-solid foundation. It also has a reputation for grooming little ones into well-rounded individuals.

The school consists of 12 classes, each class having its own reading corner. It also has a Pretend play area, Art room, Music room (A/C), sick room, and a spacious, beautiful garden with all the necessary playground equipment, a sandpit, and a swimming pool. Swimming classes – are held after school, twice a week by a very experienced swimming coach trained under late Mr. David Bolling, founder of the Bolling Swimming Academy.

Apart from learning their ABC's children also have Music, Dancing, Speech & Drama, Show & Tell, PE, Cookery, etc. on their timetables. Events include:

  • Children's Day
  • Christmas Concert at Bishop's College Auditorium
  • Independence Day
  • Sports meet at Havelocks Sports Club
  • Sinhalese and Hindu New Year
  • Vesak
  • Book Day
  • Art Exhibition

The age group and the class of admission are as follows:

  • 2 ½ to 3 ½ yrs – Play Group
  • 3 ½ to 4 ½ yrs – Nursery
  • 4 ½ to 5 ½ yrs – Kindergarten
  • 5 ½ to 6 ½ yrs – Year 1
  • 6 ½ to 7 ½ yrs – Year 2
