The AIS Alumni Association’s Incontro

Memories
📅 December 13, 2025 💬 Memories

The AIS Alumni Association’s Incontro, held on Saturday, December 13th 2025, was an unforgettable night of music, energy, and celebration.

With DJ Trev D setting the tone and a stellar live performance by Dreaming of Saturn, the atmosphere was electric from start to finish. Guests enjoyed an incredible evening at The Stables in Park Street Mews, filled with great music, vibrant company, and nonstop dancing.

Beautifully brought together with the support of valued partners, the event was a true celebration of the AIS community—leaving everyone with amazing memories and already looking forward to the next one this year.
