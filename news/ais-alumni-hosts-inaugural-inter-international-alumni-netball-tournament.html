AIS Alumni Hosts Inaugural Inter-International Alumni Netball Tournament

Memories
📅 June 1, 2026 💬 Memories

The landmark tournament brought together distinguished alumni and elite netball talent from leading international schools across Sri Lanka and abroad for a day of high-intensity competition, sportsmanship, and alumni team spirit.

Emerging as tournament champions were Honey Badgers, a premier club side comprising top players from leading international schools and players from overseas. The Asian International School Alumni secured the runners-up position, while Lyceum International School Alumni claimed third place. Colombo International School and Royal Institute completed the standings in fourth and fifth place respectively.

The tournament also recognized outstanding individual performances:

  • Netball Queen – Rushika Paulas (Honey Badgers)
  • Best Defensive Player – Upeksha Narayana (Asian International School Alumni)
  • Best Offensive Player – Risini Niriella (Honey Badgers)
  • Most Improved Player – Nethmini Silva (Lyceum International School Alumni)
  • Best Team Spirit – Royal Institute

The tournament was commended for its exceptional organization and strong focus on player welfare, professionalism, and overall experience. All matches were live-streamed, enabling supporters and audiences worldwide to witness the tournament and engage with the event remotely.

The Organizing Committee extends its sincere appreciation to the Chief Guests, Mrs. Vajira Neththicumara, President of the Sri Lanka Netball Federation, Mr. Harshana Perera, Principal of Asian International School, and Ms. Nihara Bagoos, Head of Sports at Asian International School, for their invaluable support and encouragement in making the tournament a success.

Special thanks are also extended to the Sri Lanka Air Force for its outstanding hospitality and continuous support throughout the event.

The Asian International School Alumni Association further acknowledges its valued sponsors and partners whose contributions played a vital role in the successful execution of the tournament:

  • Athilco – Trophy & Match Ball Sponsor
  • Bed & Bath – Customized Embroidered Towels
  • The Recovery Room – On-Court Recovery, Massage & Physiotherapy Support
  • SITREK Nursing and Swift Care – First Aid & Ambulance Support
  • CSL Group of Companies – Hydration Partner
  • Club Tropikai – Sportswear Partner
  • Annt Media – Creative & Communications Partner
  • Escape the Room – Entertainment Partner
  • Solo Bowl – Food Partner
  • Kiss FM – Radio Partner

The overwhelming success of the inaugural Inter-International Alumni Netball Tournament 2026 marks the beginning of what promises to become a premier annual sporting event, celebrating excellence in netball, alumni unity, and the continued growth of the international school sporting community in Sri Lanka.
