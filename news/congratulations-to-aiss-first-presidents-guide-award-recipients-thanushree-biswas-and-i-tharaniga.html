Congratulations to AIS’s first President’s Guide Award recipients, Thanushree Biswas and I. Tharaniga!

Blog
📅 July 27, 2026 💬 Blog

Congratulations to AIS’s first President’s Guide Award recipients, Thanushree Biswas and I. Tharaniga!

Your dedication, perseverance, and self-confidence have brought great honour and pride to the Girl Guide Movement at Asian International School.

We are immensely proud of your outstanding achievement in earning the highest award in Girl Guiding. May this milestone inspire many more young Guides to dream big, work hard, and serve with commitment.

Congratulations once again, and we wish you every success in your future endeavours.
