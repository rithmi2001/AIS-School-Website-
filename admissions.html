Admissions

How to join the Asian International School community.

Admission Process

A simple three-step process to join our school.

1

Step 1

You may collect the prospectus from the school office between 7.30am - 2.00pm

Documents needed:

  • A copy of the birth certificate
  • A current passport size photograph of the student

Please complete the admission form and send it to enquiries@ais.lk.

2

Step 2

Entry tests

Entry to school involves student assessments in English and Mathematics. Entry tests will take place after submitting the completed admission application. Students entering senior school (IGCSE and Advance Level classes) will have to provide a copy of their school reports.

3

Step 3

Scheduling an interview with the Principal

The final process of the admissions procedure will involve an informal interview with the principal.
